Welcome to Kuriftu Hotel Samara

A calm base in Samara, the capital of the Afar region. Comfortable rooms, honest food and a team that looks after you, whether you are here for work, a stopover or the landscape.

Rooms and suites include Wi-Fi and modern comforts. We can arrange meetings, conferences, weddings and tailor-made events, plus transport and local excursions. Tell us what you need and we will plan around it.
