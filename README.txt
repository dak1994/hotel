Kuriftu Hotel Samara website

Open index.html in a browser. Keep the folders together.

index.html        home page
rooms.html dining.html events.html recreation.html   service pages with booking forms
css/style.css     all styles (brand colours at the top)
js/common.js      menu, dialog, toast, feedback (all pages)
js/home.js        slideshow, booking bar, gallery, subscribe
js/service.js     booking form fields and checks (edit FIELDS)
images/           photos

Forms do not send data yet. Connect them to email or your booking system in js/service.js and js/home.js.
